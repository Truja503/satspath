\section{Test Documentation: Tier 1 - Core VTXO
Verification}\label{test-documentation-tier-1---core-vtxo-verification}}

\hypertarget{overview}{%
\subsection{Overview}\label{overview}}

This suite focuses on the structural and cryptographic validation of the
VTXO DAG, ensuring that every virtual transaction is authentic and
correctly anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain.

\hypertarget{test-goals}{%
\subsubsection{🎯 Test Goals}\label{test-goals}}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{DAG Reconstruction}: Programmatically building the
  ancestor-child relationship from the \textbf{VTXO Root} down to the
  commitment anchor.
\item
  \textbf{Signature Integrity}: Verifying that every transaction in the
  DAG was signed by the appropriate MuSig2/Schnorr aggregated keys
  (including internal and tweaked keys).
\item
  \textbf{On-chain Anchor}: Confirming the \textbf{Anchoring Leaves} of
  the virtual tree are correctly anchored to a confirmed commitment
  transaction.
\end{enumerate}

\hypertarget{environment}{%
\subsubsection{🌐 Environment}\label{environment}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Providers}: \texttt{MockIndexerProvider} and
  \texttt{MockOnchainProvider}.
\item
  \textbf{Data Source}: Simulated Regtest/Signet transaction structures
  (PSBTs and on-chain status).
\item
  \textbf{Execution}: Node.js/Vitest environment.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Valid DAG} & A consistent 3-level Reversed-DAG with correct
signatures and confirmed commitment. & \textbf{PASS} \\
\textbf{Tampered Sig} & A single bit change in the Schnorr signature of
an ancestor node. & \textbf{FAIL (INVALID\_SIGNATURE)} \\
\textbf{Missing Link} & A DAG where a child input doesn't correctly
reference its ancestor's outpoint. & \textbf{FAIL
(INPUT\_CHAIN\_BREAK)} \\
\textbf{Ghost Commitment} & A valid DAG whose anchoring commitment is
not found on-chain. & \textbf{FAIL (COMMITMENT\_NOT\_FOUND)} \\
\textbf{Anti-Mirage Attack} & ASP provides a fake TXID or malformed hex
during anchoring. & \textbf{FAIL (ORACLE\_POISONING\_DETECTED)} \\
\textbf{RPC Spoofing} & Bitcoin RPC returns HTTP 200 with result: null
or malformed JSON. & \textbf{FAIL (INVALID\_RPC\_RESPONSE)} \\
\end{longtable}
